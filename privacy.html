Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
Plain-English summary, provided in good faith. This is a starting template and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be finalised before public launch.

Jackl ("we", "us") is a dog-owner app for walks, dog profiles and a local dog community. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Jackl you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Jackl is operated from Australia. For any privacy question or request, contact us at ops@jacklpack.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely and hashed, never in plain text).
  • Dog profiles: your dog's name, breed, age, photos, bio, vibe words, and any health or vaccination details you choose to add.
  • Location: while you use the walk map we access your device location to show your position, record a walk route, show nearby dogs, and power lost-dog alerts. We store a coarse "home area" (rounded to roughly a 1km area, never your exact address) so we can notify you of lost dogs near where you walk.
  • Activity: walks, distances, check-ins, badges, friends, packs and messages you create in the app.
  • Notifications: a device push token so we can send the alerts you have turned on.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash and error reports to help us fix problems.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: your Dog Tag, walks, badges, and the local features.
  • To connect you with other dogs and owners you choose to interact with.
  • To send lost and found dog alerts to people near a missing dog, and to notify you of activity you have opted into.
  • To keep the app safe (for example, handling reports of misuse) and to fix bugs.

What other people can see

Jackl is social by design, but you control most of it:

  • Your dog profile and Dog Tag are visible to other users you connect with.
  • Your live location on the map is only shared when you turn live-sharing on, and you can be invisible.
  • A lost or found dog alert you post is shown publicly to nearby users, including any contact number you add, because the goal is to reunite the dog quickly. Only post what you are comfortable sharing.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted providers to run the app, including Supabase (secure database, authentication and file storage) and Expo (push notifications). Map tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and associated data. Some information may remain in backups or logs for a limited period, or where we are required to keep it by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and its data from inside the app, in Settings.
  • Location: you can turn location off in your device settings (some features will not work without it).
  • Notifications: you can turn categories on or off in the app, or off entirely in device settings.
  • Access or correction: contact us at ops@jacklpack.com and we will help.

Children

Jackl is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service can be completely secure. Please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes we will let you know in the app or by email. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: ops@jacklpack.com.
